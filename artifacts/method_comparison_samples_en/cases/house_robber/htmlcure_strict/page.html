🏠 House Robber

Maximize loot without triggering adjacent alarms

📋 Problem Statement

A thief plans to rob houses along a street. Each house i contains nums[i] cash. Adjacent houses have connected alarms — robbing two adjacent houses triggers the alarm. Find the maximum total cash that can be robbed safely.

Input array: nums = [2, 7, 9, 3, 1]

i=0 🏠 $2 i=1 🏠 $7 i=2 🏠 $9 i=3 🏠 $3 i=4 🏠 $1
